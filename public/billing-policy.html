1

Platform Subscriptions

Companies may subscribe to paid FairLinx platform plans. Prices, plan limits, billing cycles, renewal dates, and included features are shown in the billing dashboard, pricing page, or checkout flow.

2

Payment Provider

Platform subscription payments may be processed through Stripe or another approved third-party payment provider. Additional payment provider terms, fees, verification requirements, or processing rules may apply.

3

Automatic Renewals

Unless cancelled or changed through the available billing controls, subscriptions may renew automatically at the end of each billing cycle using the payment method on file.

4

Failed Payments and Past-Due Accounts

If a payment fails, the company account may enter a past-due or warning state. FairLinx may retry payment, request updated billing information, limit selected paid features, or restrict account access according to the applicable plan and product configuration.

5

Plan Changes

Companies may be able to upgrade, downgrade, or cancel plans through supported billing controls. Changes may take effect immediately, at the end of the current billing cycle, or according to the rules shown during checkout.

6

Taxes

Displayed prices may not include applicable taxes unless clearly stated. Companies are responsible for taxes, reporting obligations, and business-related charges associated with their own operations.

7

Marketplace Payments

FairLinx platform subscription billing is separate from payments between customers and service companies. Unless otherwise stated, FairLinx does not process customer job payments, hold contractor funds, or take a commission from service work arranged outside the platform subscription.

8

Contact

For billing questions, contact FairLinx support through your account dashboard or email: support@fairlinx.app

Our support team is here to help you with any billing questions or account issues you may have.
