Frequently asked questions

Common questions from landscaping and outdoor service operators evaluating FairLinx.

Is FairLinx only for landscaping?

FairLinx is built for landscaping first, but also supports broader outdoor service businesses with similar workflow and crew operations.

Does FairLinx take commission?

No. FairLinx does not take a percentage of completed jobs. The platform uses company subscriptions.

Can workers use it on mobile?

Yes. FairLinx is mobile friendly so field teams can check assignments and status updates from phones and tablets.

What happens when a marketplace request is accepted?

Accepted marketplace work converts into your SaaS workflow so you can manage it as lead, estimate, and job inside your normal operations process.

Can I start with the demo/free plan?

Yes. You can start on Starter and move to Pro as your team and workflow needs grow.
