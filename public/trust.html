What Verified Business means

A verified business completed FairLinx verification steps based on available company records and platform checks.

What Insured means

An insured badge indicates the business has supplied insurance information with a currently valid status on the platform.

What Licensed means

A licensed badge indicates the business submitted licensing information with an approved and non-expired status where applicable.

How reports and moderation work

FairLinx reviews reports and moderation signals to help reduce harmful behavior. Safety and policy checks are ongoing and may update visibility or trust indicators.

What FairLinx does NOT do

  • FairLinx does not handle payments between customers and companies.
  • FairLinx does not take commission from completed jobs.
  • FairLinx does not guarantee service outcomes or workmanship.

Customer safety tips

  • Confirm scope, pricing, and timeline in writing before work starts.
  • Review trust badges, service coverage, and recent reviews together.
  • Verify company contact details and business identity directly.
  • Keep records of messages, estimates, and final agreements.
