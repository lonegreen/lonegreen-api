Trust Model Notes

Trust signals support decisions with clear platform boundaries

FairLinx transparency tools help customers evaluate companies while preserving direct customer-to-company service relationships.

  • FairLinx does not take commission from completed jobs.
  • FairLinx does not process customer-to-company job payments.
  • Trust badges and reviews support decisions but do not replace direct due diligence.
