1. Roles

For company customer data, the company is generally the controller or business, and LoneGreen acts as a processor or service provider operating the SaaS platform.

2. Processing Instructions

We process company customer data to provide the platform, maintain security, troubleshoot, support authorized users, and comply with legal requirements.

3. Subprocessors

Hosting, database, email, payment, and monitoring providers may process data as subprocessors. A production policy should list provider names and locations after attorney review.

4. Security Measures

The platform uses authentication, role-based access, company isolation, database controls, and operational logging. Companies remain responsible for user management and lawful data entry.

5. Deletion and Export

Authorized company owners or admins may request data export or deletion subject to verification, retention obligations, and technical feasibility.
