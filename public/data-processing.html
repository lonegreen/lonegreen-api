1. Roles

For company customer data, the company is generally the controller or business, and FairLinx acts as a processor or service provider operating the SaaS platform on behalf of the company.

2. Processing Instructions

FairLinx processes company customer data to provide the platform, maintain security, troubleshoot issues, support authorized users, operate workflow features, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Subprocessors

Hosting, database, email, payment, monitoring, infrastructure, and support providers may process data as subprocessors where necessary to operate the platform. FairLinx should maintain an internal subprocessor list for production operations and update this policy after legal review.

4. Security Measures

The platform uses authentication, role-based access, company isolation, database controls, operational logging, and administrative safeguards designed to protect account and workflow data. Companies remain responsible for user management, lawful data entry, permission assignments, and appropriate account access controls.

5. Deletion and Export

Authorized company owners or admins may request data export or deletion subject to identity verification, retention obligations, legal requirements, customer obligations, and technical feasibility.

6. Contact

For questions about this Data Processing Policy, contact FairLinx at privacy@fairlinx.com.
