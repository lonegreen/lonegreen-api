# Launch Marketing Assets

## Company Onboarding Messaging
- "Run leads, jobs, invoices, and recurring services in one platform."
- Emphasize faster operations and less workflow fragmentation.

## Customer Acquisition Messaging
- "Find trusted local service companies quickly."
- Emphasize easier request flow and transparent trust signals.

## Founding Partner Messaging
- "Early partners shape local launch quality and operational standards."
- Highlight direct visibility and platform-level support.

## Trust Positioning
- Focus on verification status, service coverage, and response quality.
- Present reliability and consistency over hype claims.

## No-Commission Positioning
- "Keep your job revenue; no commission cuts."
- Position as a sustainable model for local service companies.

## SaaS Pricing Positioning
- "Predictable monthly software pricing for operational control."
- Contrast with variable lead-fee models.

## TikTok Ad Angle
- Short-form pain-to-solution sequence: "From scattered tools to one workflow."
- Showcase before/after daily operations for local service owners.

## Local Outreach Angle
- Neighborhood-first campaign with service categories and trust signals.
- Partner with local business groups and service communities.

## Referral Angle
- Encourage customer-to-customer and company-to-company referrals.
- Position referrals as trust accelerators in local markets.
