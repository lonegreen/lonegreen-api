# FairLinx Founding Partner Onboarding

## Partner Eligibility
- Licensed/insured service company with clear service scope.
- Willingness to maintain profile quality and response SLAs.
- Acceptance of platform trust, moderation, and support standards.

## Onboarding Steps
1. Company registration and admin account setup.
2. Public profile completion (description, logo, contact details).
3. Service category mapping.
4. Service area setup and validation.
5. Availability/hours setup.
6. Verification submission and review.
7. Trial/billing readiness confirmation.
8. First lead/request handling walkthrough.

## Company Profile Setup
- Complete public description with accurate service details.
- Add valid contact channels and branding assets.
- Confirm discover/profile rendering quality.

## Service Categories Setup
- Select core categories that match real fulfillment capability.
- Avoid over-broad category activation.
- Validate marketplace matching relevance.

## Service Areas Setup
- Add realistic coverage areas and radius values.
- Verify zip/city/state mapping.
- Recheck discover and request matching behavior.

## Verification Checklist
- Business identity documentation submitted.
- License/insurance status updated.
- Trust statuses reviewed by platform owner.
- Public trust badges validated.

## Billing / Free Trial Handling
- Confirm trial start/end expectations with partner.
- Explain mutation restrictions when billing is restricted.
- Provide billing support escalation path.

## Support Expectations
- Use support tickets for operational issues.
- Use disputes for conflict review workflows (non-refund by default).
- Response-time expectations documented.

## Launch Feedback Loop
- Weekly check-ins for first launch cohort.
- Track: request quality, conversion friction, support load.
- Feed top issues into roadmap and launch-gate hardening.
